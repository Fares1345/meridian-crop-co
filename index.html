05 / The Meridian method

Nothing important is invisible.

Our sourcing standard follows each lot through four gates. Data supports the cup; it never replaces it.

01

At origin

Field selection

Agronomy, ripeness, separation, and producer economics are evaluated before a sample travels.

02

At the lab

Blind calibration

Three Q-graders cup every offer blind across two roast profiles, then reconcile sensory and physical data.

03

In transit

Protected passage

Moisture-stable liners, route monitoring, and staged arrival checks guard the coffee through export.

04

In your roastery

Open continuity

Responsive samples, roast context, and transparent replacement terms keep quality intact after delivery.
